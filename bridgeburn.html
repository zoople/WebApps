How to play
  • Drag a card onto your half of the arena, or tap a card and then tap where it should land. Keys 1–4 pick cards.
  • Cards cost elixir, which refills over time. The last minute runs at double speed. If crowns are level at 3:00, sudden death starts at 3× and climbs every minute (4×, 5×…) until someone takes a tower.
  • A side tower is worth one crown. The king tower is worth three and ends the match.
  • Knock down a side tower and you can deploy deeper on that side of their half.
  • The rival's difficulty changes how fast it reacts and how much elixir it gets: Easy is slow, Normal is fair, Hard gets extra.
  • Academy has eleven drills, each teaching one Classic Challenge skill over three rounds, plus a field guide. After every match the coach grades your play and points you to the drill that fixes your biggest mistake. Turn on Live coach on the start screen to get feedback on each play as it happens.
  • Classic Challenge plays like the real one: keep going until 8 wins or 3 losses. The Rival gets tougher as you win.
