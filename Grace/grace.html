Grace

Give up power. Keep your grace.

How it works
  • Drag anywhere to move (arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard). You fire on your own.
  • Each floor is three waves and a Warden. Enemies come from above and below, and they never change. You do.
  • Grace ✦ drains every second you fight. Every blessing you hold costs upkeep, and the Legendary ones cost the most.
  • After each Warden an offering is owed: give up one blessing. A Legendary, a Rare and a Common are on offer.
  • Sacrifices restore Grace: Legendary +110, Rare +60, Common +30. Grace caps at 130; anything over still counts toward your score.
  • Walk over ✦ sparks for Grace (+8) and ❤️ hearts to heal (never above full health).
  • Tap any blessing icon at the top to see what it does and what it costs.
  • At zero Grace 💀 your health drains fast until you find more.
  • Moving stone slabs block every shot, yours and theirs. Use them for cover.
  • Sometimes you can Refuse and keep everything, but each refusal doubles your drain for good.
  • The last floor is fought with nothing. Clear it, and the Grace you have left is your score.
